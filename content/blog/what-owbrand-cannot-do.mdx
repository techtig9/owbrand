## Not built

- **Finished video.** Reel *scripting* works — beats, pacing, the order your
  photos should appear in. Rendering that into a clip is not built, so there is
  no file at the end.
- **One-click deploy.** Site content exports as an archive. Deploying it to
  Vercel or Netlify from inside owbrand is not built; those endpoints say so
  explicitly rather than starting a deployment that never finishes.
- **A visual site builder.** Site content is generated and edited as content,
  not dragged around a canvas.

## Waiting on someone else

- **Instagram and Facebook publishing** needs Meta App Review for the
  publishing permissions, which takes two to six weeks. Until it is granted, an
  account connects but reports that it needs reconnecting, and publishing is
  refused rather than queued into a void.
- **TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest and X** each need their own approved
  app. None are supported today, and each refuses specifically rather than
  accepting a post it cannot send.

## Why this page exists

Every one of these is discoverable by signing up and trying. Finding it that
way costs you an afternoon and costs us a refund.
