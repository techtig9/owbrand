## The problem with prompting

Ask a model for "a post about our new product, friendly but not casual" and you
get something reasonable. Ask again next week, with slightly different wording,
and you get something else reasonable. Both are fine in isolation. Neither is
your brand, because there is no *your brand* anywhere in the system — only two
prompts that happened to be typed by the same person.

## What gets stored

A Brand Brain holds the things you would otherwise retype:

- **Voice** — the adjectives, and the things you never say.
- **Audience** — who is being addressed, and what they already know.
- **Positioning** — what you are, and what you are deliberately not.
- **Prohibited claims** — the statements that must never appear, whatever the
  prompt asks for.

Every generation reads it. That is the whole mechanism.

## Why prohibited claims are part of it

Most brand tools treat safety as a filter applied after generation. Storing the
claims you are *not* allowed to make alongside the ones you are means the
constraint travels with the brand, so it applies to copy nobody thought to
review.
