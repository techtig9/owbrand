Credits meter AI generation. Everything else in the product is free.

## What uses a credit

Generating anything with a model: brand building, copy, captions, creative
briefs, recommendations, image analysis.

## What does not

- **Publishing and scheduling.** Sending a post to a platform costs nothing,
  so a post scheduled while you had credits still publishes after they run out.
- **The consistency check.** It is deterministic, not a model call.
- **Analytics.** Fetching your metrics is not a generation.
- **The brand-kit export**, the calendar, the API, and everything in settings.

## When you get a credit back

Automatically, and immediately, when a generation:

- errors,
- times out, or
- returns output that fails validation.

You are never charged for a generation you did not receive. The credit ledger
on your Billing page shows every reservation and refund with the reason, so you
can check this rather than take our word for it.

## What is not refunded

A generation that completed successfully, including one whose output you did
not like. It cost us money either way, and "I did not like it" is not
distinguishable from "I changed my mind" by anything we can measure.

## Credits do not roll over

Monthly credits reset on renewal. Unused ones are not carried forward.
