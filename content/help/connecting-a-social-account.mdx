## What connecting actually does

You are redirected to the platform, you approve specific permissions, and the
platform hands us an access token. We encrypt that token before storing it, and
use it only to publish what you schedule and to read metrics for your own
posts.

We never post anything you did not schedule.

## Why the button might be unavailable

Publishing to Instagram and Facebook needs a Meta app with approved
permissions. If this deployment does not have one, the connection is shown as
unavailable rather than failing halfway through the flow — a button that starts
an OAuth redirect and dies at the callback is worse than one that says it is
not ready.

## Why your account may say "needs reconnect"

Access tokens expire. When one does, we mark the account and stop attempting to
publish to it, because every attempt would fail identically and fill your queue
with noise.

Reconnecting takes a few seconds and does not affect anything already published.

## If the operator rotates the encryption key

Every stored token becomes unreadable and all accounts report that they need
reconnecting. This is intended behaviour, not a fault: the alternative is an
encryption key that can never be rotated.

## What we do not store

Your platform password, obviously — we never see it. We also do not include
access tokens in your data export, even though they belong to you, because a
plaintext credential in a downloaded file is a worse outcome than omitting it.
