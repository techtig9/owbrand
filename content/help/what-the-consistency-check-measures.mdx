The consistency check on the Brand Kit page takes any copy and tells you where
it departs from your brand. Here is exactly what it does.

## What it checks

- **Words on your avoid list.** Whole-word, case-insensitive. Each one is a
  blocker.
- **Claims nothing may make** — medical claims, guarantees, absolutes. These
  are the same checks that run on generated copy before it can be published.
- **Your writing rules**, where they state something countable. A rule saying
  "keep sentences under 20 words" is enforced; one saying "be friendly" is not,
  because nothing can measure it.
- **Your preferred words**, as a suggestion on longer passages.
- **Two style tells**: heavy exclamation use, and hedging.

## What a score of 100 means

**It means nothing checkable is wrong. It does not mean the copy is on brand.**

This is the most important sentence on this page. The check cannot tell you
whether writing is any good, whether it will land with your audience, or
whether it says the right thing. It tells you that it breaks none of the rules
you have written down.

That is genuinely useful — it catches the banned word in the fourth paragraph
that everyone skims past — and it is much narrower than "on brand".

## Why the score is shown with a coverage figure

Next to every score you will see something like *checked against 2 of 5 brand
rules*. If your Brand Brain has no avoid list and no writing rules, there is
almost nothing to check against, and a 100 in that situation would be
misleading. The coverage figure is there so the number can never be read on its
own.

The fix is to add rules. Every avoid word and every writing rule you add makes
the check sharper.

## Why it is not an AI judgement

We could ask a model "is this on brand?" and show you a percentage. We do not,
for three reasons:

1. **It would be unfalsifiable.** "82% on brand" cannot be argued with or acted
   on. "The word *utilise* is on your avoid list, in this sentence" can be
   fixed in ten seconds.
2. **It would cost credits**, so you would ration it — and a check you ration
   is a check you stop using.
3. **It would not be stable.** The same copy would score differently on
   different days, and a number that drifts teaches people to ignore it.

The check is free and unlimited because it is deterministic. That is the trade,
and we think it is the right one.
