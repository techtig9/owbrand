A post in the queue shows **Failed**. There are four causes, and they need
different things from you.

## 1. The connection expired

The most common one. Meta access tokens expire, and when yours does, nothing
publishes to that account until you reconnect.

**How to tell:** the Connections page shows the account as *Needs reconnect*.

**What to do:** reconnect it. Posts that failed for this reason are not retried
automatically once the token is gone — requeue them from the queue after
reconnecting.

## 2. The platform rejected the media

Instagram is strict about aspect ratios, video length and file size, and it
rejects rather than adjusts. The queue shows the platform's own message,
verbatim, because ours would be a worse version of it.

**What to do:** fix the media and schedule again. This is a permanent failure —
retrying the same file produces the same rejection, so we do not retry it.

## 3. The platform was down

Temporary. We retry five times with increasing gaps, and most of these succeed
on the second or third attempt without you seeing anything.

If all five fail, the post moves to a state where we can replay it once the
platform recovers. You do not need to do anything; if it stays failed for more
than a day, contact us.

## 4. Nothing is running the publisher

The one that catches self-hosted deployments. Publishing needs a scheduled job
calling `/api/cron/publish`. Without it, posts sit in the queue looking
scheduled and nothing ever sends them.

**How to tell:** the Scheduler page shows a banner saying publishing is paused.
If you see that banner, none of the above applies — nothing is failing, nothing
is running.

## Why we do not just retry everything

Retrying a post the platform permanently rejected produces the identical
rejection, burns the retry budget, and hides the real failure behind four
duplicates. So we separate "the platform was broken" from "this cannot be
published", and only replay the first.
